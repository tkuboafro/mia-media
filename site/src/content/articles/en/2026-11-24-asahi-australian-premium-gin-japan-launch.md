## A Major Brewer Brings Australian Craft Gin to Japan

Asahi Breweries, one of Japan's major alcoholic drinks makers, is to launch gin from the Australian distillery Never Never Distilling in Japan. It goes on sale on 25 November 2026. The day before, on the 24th, a press conference was held in Tokyo to present the distillery's flagship product, Triple Juniper Gin. Juniper (the berry of the common juniper) is the basic botanical that defines a gin's aroma. This gin, which carries the name in its title, looks set to be the centrepiece of the Japanese launch.

## Targeting Growing Demand for Premium Gin, and Exports Beyond

Asahi is aiming at the growing demand for higher-priced gin in Japan. In Japan's alcoholic drinks market, beer, sake and whisky tend to attract most of the attention, but gin has become a category that the major players are putting effort into. By taking on an overseas premium brand, Asahi can also be seen as trying to establish an early foothold in a growing market.

According to Nikkei Asia, if its efforts in Japan go well, Asahi is also looking at exports to the United States, Europe and elsewhere. A major Japanese company building up an overseas gin brand at home and then taking it to the world is a notable new pattern for the overseas expansion of Japan's drinks industry.

Europe is often called the home of gin, and many readers will be familiar with dry styles with a strong juniper character. What is worth watching in this move is how an Australian premium gin will be received in Japan, one of Asia's largest alcoholic drinks markets. If Asahi's export plan goes ahead, gin that has passed through the hands of a major Japanese company may one day appear on European shelves. Until then, you could compare juniper-forward gins that are available to you, as well as the Japanese craft gins now turning up in Europe, either neat to appreciate the aroma or mixed with tonic.
