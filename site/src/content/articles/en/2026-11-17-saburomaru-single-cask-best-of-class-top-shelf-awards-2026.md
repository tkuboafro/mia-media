In Toyama Prefecture, on the Sea of Japan coast in central Honshu, a whisky from Saburomaru Distillery, run by Wakatsuru Shuzo Co., Ltd., has won an international award. At the Top Shelf Awards 2026 ceremony held in San Francisco on 8 November 2026 (local time), "Single Cask Saburomaru Zhuge Liang #210153" was named Best of Class, the top prize in the Japanese whisky category.

## A year of acclaim crowned as the category's best

<figure class="embed embed--image"><img src="https://prcdn.freetls.fastly.net/release_image/31708/208/31708-208-fa1eeee0e076888542bb83a55f4829dd-3900x2601.jpg?width=1200&amp;height=900" alt="Official photo announcing Saburomaru Distillery&#x27;s award" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>Official photo announcing Saburomaru Distillery&#x27;s award — Image: PR TIMES (press release)</figcaption></figure>

The Top Shelf Awards are organised by The Tasting Alliance, a US company that runs international spirits competitions. It holds five competitions, including the San Francisco World Spirits Competition (SFWSC). The awards honour brands that have scored highly across those competitions over the course of the year, and each year they announce a Best of Class for every category as well as an overall top prize, Best in Show, among others. The winning bottle received the highest rating, Double Gold, at the SFWSC in June 2026, and has now gone one step further to take the category's top award.

## 580 bottles from a single cask

<figure class="embed embed--image"><img src="https://prcdn.freetls.fastly.net/release_image/31708/208/31708-208-cbec096bf98492d51dc5ed1090292292-142x373.png?width=1200&amp;height=900" alt="The winning bottle, &quot;Single Cask Saburomaru Zhuge Liang #210153&quot;" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>The winning bottle, &quot;Single Cask Saburomaru Zhuge Liang #210153&quot; — Image: PR TIMES (press release)</figcaption></figure>

A "single cask" whisky is one bottled from the contents of a single cask, with no blending of different casks. This bottle was distilled in 2021, matured in a Spanish oak cask that had previously held oloroso, a type of sherry, and bottled in 2024. Its strength is 59% ABV, and it was released at that high strength without being diluted with water. The run came to 580 bottles. It went on sale in March 2025 as one of four products (in all) in a collaboration with Romance of the Three Kingdoms 8 Remake, a historical simulation game from the Japanese games company Koei Tecmo Games, and the cask was chosen to match the image of Zhuge Liang, the strategist of China's Three Kingdoms period. The recommended retail price in Japan was 27,000 yen (excluding tax), and it has already sold out.

## Over 70 years devoted to smokiness

<figure class="embed embed--image"><img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1571104508999-893933ded431?crop=entropy&amp;cs=tinysrgb&amp;fit=max&amp;fm=jpg&amp;ixid=M3wxMDg1MTk5fDB8MXxzZWFyY2h8MXx8cGVhdGVkJTIwd2hpc2t5JTIwZ2xhc3N8ZW58MXwwfHx8MTc5NDk1MTU0NXww&amp;ixlib=rb-4.1.0&amp;q=80&amp;w=1080" alt="A tasting glass filled with amber whisky" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>A tasting glass filled with amber whisky — Photo: <a href="https://unsplash.com/@thomascpark?utm_source=the_sake_wire&amp;utm_medium=referral" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Thomas Park</a> (<a href="https://unsplash.com/?utm_source=the_sake_wire&amp;utm_medium=referral" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Unsplash</a>)</figcaption></figure>

Saburomaru Distillery, whose motto is "to master smokiness", says it has been making whisky with a smoky aroma for more than 70 years. The distillery sees this recognition as proof that judges around the world have acknowledged that long commitment.

Because the winning bottle has already sold out, it will be hard to find in Europe. Even so, the Saburomaru name is worth remembering. If you spot "Saburomaru" on the list of a whisky specialist or an importer of Japanese whisky, it may be worth a closer look. Smoky spirit matured in sherry casks is said to sometimes show a dried-fruit-like sweetness beneath the smoke. With a high-strength single cask, adding a little water is said to change the impression of the aroma.
