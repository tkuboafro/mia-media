## Gold chosen in a blind tasting by women judges from several countries

<figure class="embed embed--image"><img src="https://prcdn.freetls.fastly.net/release_image/135255/14/135255-14-1758574b278e8c1b56cb348d435bb4cc-1254x1254.png?width=1200&amp;height=900" alt="A tasting set of three Fermenteria sakes, including the gold-winning &quot;CITRÈNE Japan Yuzu × Sansho&quot;" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>A tasting set of three Fermenteria sakes, including the gold-winning &quot;CITRÈNE Japan Yuzu × Sansho&quot; — Image: PR TIMES (press release)</figcaption></figure>

"CITRÈNE Japan Yuzu × Sansho", made by the small brewery "SENDAI STATION BREWERY Fermenteria" inside the station in Sendai, the main city of the Tohoku region, won gold in the low-alcohol category of the "4th Bishu Competition 2026". The competition judges craft sake as well as traditional seishu. In 2026, women judges from Japan, Taiwan, South Korea, Singapore, the United States, Australia and Switzerland assessed the appearance, aroma and taste of 312 entries with the labels hidden. This was the only craft sake to win gold.

Craft sake is made by adding fruit, herbs and other ingredients while rice is fermented, and it is a new style that has been spreading in Japan in recent years. CITRÈNE is brewed from Miyagi-grown Sasanishiki rice with a polishing ratio (the proportion of the brown rice left after milling) of 60%. Yuzu and young sansho berries, which have a fresh, citrus-like aroma, are added, and fermentation turns the aroma into a cool, floral impression. No lactic acid is added; white koji and the citric acid from the yuzu give a light acidity. Natural bubbles are captured by secondary fermentation in the bottle, and the alcohol content is 7%.

## Thirty 8-litre tanks, brewing a little every day

<figure class="embed embed--image"><img src="https://prcdn.freetls.fastly.net/release_image/135255/14/135255-14-755ac7d6e89ce3adf0484200bf8ac2bd-1600x1075.jpg?width=1200&amp;height=900" alt="The Fermenteria brewery on the ground floor of Sendai Station" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>The Fermenteria brewery on the ground floor of Sendai Station — Image: PR TIMES (press release)</figcaption></figure>

The company behind it is Katsuhanakura Co., Ltd. (Sendai, Miyagi Prefecture), founded in 2023. At the brewery, which opened on the ground floor of Sendai Station in 2024, thirty small 8-litre tanks are used in parallel to brew a wide range of sakes in small batches every day. Because the batches are small, the cycle of trial, testing and improvement can be run quickly, and the award-winning sake came out of that accumulated effort. With Sendai as its first model, the company says it wants to spread breweries that make sake from local ingredients to other parts of Japan and to cities overseas.

## Number one in sales in Hong Kong, and looking for overseas partners

<figure class="embed embed--image"><img src="https://images.pexels.com/photos/8084660/pexels-photo-8084660.jpeg?auto=compress&amp;cs=tinysrgb&amp;dpr=2&amp;h=650&amp;w=940" alt="Lightly sparkling craft sake in a white wine glass" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>Lightly sparkling craft sake in a white wine glass — Photo: Ivan S (Pexels)</figcaption></figure>

At "Sake Jump Hong Kong 2026", held from 26 to 28 June 2026, 22 breweries showed more than 100 labels, and over 2,500 visitors attended across the three days. Fermenteria exhibited three sakes, including the award winner, and the three together ranked first among the exhibiting breweries in both booth sales and bottle sales. The other two are "Fermenteria Original", a lightly sparkling sake inspired by the young moromi (fermenting mash) of a junmai ginjo (a premium sake made from only rice and rice koji, fermented slowly at low temperature), and "CITRÈNE Taiwan Lime × Magao", made with lime and magao, a spice from the mountains of Taiwan. The company is currently looking for overseas importers and distributors.

In Europe, a good way to find new-style sakes like this is to look first for terms such as "craft sake", "low alcohol" and "bottle-fermented". If you come across a type like CITRÈNE, serve it well chilled in a white wine glass and follow how the citrus aroma of a freshly opened bottle shifts towards a floral one as the bubbles settle. At just 7% alcohol it is light and also works as an aperitif. The freshness of yuzu and sansho should pair well with light dishes made with seafood or herbs.
