Umeshu is a traditional Japanese liqueur made by steeping ume (a Japanese fruit close to the apricot) in distilled spirit and sugar. Ooyama Yumekobo Co., Ltd., a specialist umeshu maker in Ooyama-machi, Hita, Oita Prefecture, on the island of Kyushu, has won gold in the Premium Umeshu category of the "National Umeshu Competition 2026", organised by the Umeshu Research Association, for its "Taru-jikomi Koukyu Umeshu Yume Hibiki" (barrel-brewed premium umeshu). The announcement was issued as a press release by Kyushu Railway Company (JR Kyushu). This is the second gold for the product, following one in 2015. In addition, "3-Year Aged Umeshu Yume Hibiki" took silver in the White Liquor Umeshu category.

## Depth from five or more years of ageing

The gold-winning umeshu is made by first producing a base liqueur aged for at least three years, then resting it in barrels for a further two years. The process takes five years or more in total. The rich umami of the ume, drawn out by the long ageing, is combined with the full aroma imparted by the barrel, producing a refined taste with depth, the company says. There are only three ingredients: brewing alcohol, ume and sugar. Because the ingredients are so simple, the company explains, it takes great care over every stage, from selecting the raw materials to brewing and ageing. The Premium Umeshu category is open to all umeshu priced above a certain level, regardless of base spirit or production method. The entry was judged alongside distinctive high-end products such as sake-based umeshu.

## The rare Oshukubai ume and the Ooyama growing area

<figure class="embed embed--image"><img src="https://prcdn.freetls.fastly.net/release_image/37933/1472/37933-1472-c6e9363b07025387fdcf484cf62a8ed0-479x321.jpg?width=1200&amp;height=900" alt="&quot;3-Year Aged Umeshu Yume Hibiki&quot;, made with green Oshukubai ume" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>&quot;3-Year Aged Umeshu Yume Hibiki&quot;, made with green Oshukubai ume — Image: PR TIMES（九州旅客鉄道） (press release)</figcaption></figure>

The silver-winning 3-year aged product uses Oshukubai, a rare, long-established Japanese variety. It has a rich acidity and a refined aroma, making it well suited to umeshu. Only carefully selected, high-quality green ume from Oshukubai trees grown locally in Ooyama are used. This 3-year aged base liqueur also forms the base of the gold-winning barrel-brewed umeshu. White liquor, incidentally, is a distilled spirit with a neutral flavour that is widely used for making fruit liqueurs.

## Eleven consecutive awards since the first competition

<figure class="embed embed--image"><img src="https://prcdn.freetls.fastly.net/release_image/37933/1472/37933-1472-3184f757dd6a6aeca3a85dc80b0c03e4-546x416.jpg?width=1200&amp;height=900" alt="The umeshu range produced by Ooyama Yumekobo" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>The umeshu range produced by Ooyama Yumekobo — Image: PR TIMES（九州旅客鉄道） (press release)</figcaption></figure>

The National Umeshu Competition began in 2015 with the aim of improving the quality of umeshu and spreading its culture. Entries are judged on aroma, flavour and balance. Ooyama Yumekobo has won an award at every competition held since the first, and this year marks its eleventh consecutive win (no competition was held in 2020 because of the pandemic). Another brand, "Baika Ranman Premium", has won gold three times and was inducted into the Hall of Fame in 2023.

When choosing umeshu in Europe, look for long-aged types whose labels say "aged" or "barrel". They offer a complexity closer to brandy or sherry than to a merely sweet liqueur. We recommend sipping it slowly after a meal, poured into a small glass at room temperature or over ice. If you can get hold of both the 3-year aged and barrel-aged versions, you can compare them side by side and hear clearly how the barrel changes the aroma and mouthfeel.
