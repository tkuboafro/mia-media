## Two Retiring Icons and "Japan's First Craft Beer"

<figure class="embed embed--image"><img src="https://prcdn.freetls.fastly.net/release_image/95753/2480/95753-2480-b3722fcf8f529ddf16896164453fc956-1280x720.png?width=1200&amp;height=900" alt="Product image of THE LAST RUN BEER, commemorating the retirement of the 500 Series Shinkansen and the Type 923 Doctor Yellow" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>Product image of THE LAST RUN BEER, commemorating the retirement of the 500 Series Shinkansen and the Type 923 Doctor Yellow — Image: PR TIMES（西日本旅客鉄道） (press release)</figcaption></figure>

JR West Japan Business Co., Ltd., the trading company of the West Japan Railway Company (JR West) Group, and Echigo Beer, a brewery in Niigata, Niigata Prefecture, will release the canned beer "THE LAST RUN BEER" on 27 November 2026. It commemorates two Shinkansen trains due to retire in January 2027. One is the "500 Series Shinkansen", the first train in Japan to achieve commercial operation at 300 km/h. The other is the "Type 923 Doctor Yellow", a yellow inspection train that runs while measuring the condition of the track and equipment, and has supported the safety of the Shinkansen for many years. Both trains are popular not only with railway enthusiasts but also with families. Echigo Beer is known as the brewery that produced "Japan's first craft beer", and this is its first joint project with the JR West Group. The cans feature the official retirement posters of both trains and the JR West Group logo, in a design you will want to keep as a souvenir even after the beer is gone.

## A Classic Pilsner Scented with Czech Hops

<figure class="embed embed--image"><img src="https://images.pexels.com/photos/7016487/pexels-photo-7016487.jpeg?auto=compress&amp;cs=tinysrgb&amp;dpr=2&amp;h=650&amp;w=940" alt="A clear, golden pilsner (illustrative image)" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>A clear, golden pilsner (illustrative image) — Photo: Darlene Alderson (Pexels)</figcaption></figure>

The beer inside is a pilsner. Pilsner is a pale golden, crisp style of lager that originated in the Bohemia region of Czechia. This beer uses only malt as its raw material, along with aroma hops from the Czech Saaz region (the name used in English- and German-speaking countries). Many of Japan's big-brand beers use rice or corn as adjuncts, so "100% malt" is often used as a label that signals a commitment to quality. It is said to be characterised by a showy hop aroma and a refreshing bitterness. The beer has an alcohol content of 5%, comes in a 350 ml can and costs 374 yen including tax in Japan.

## For European Readers: How to Enjoy It

<figure class="embed embed--image"><img src="https://images.pexels.com/photos/31985625/pexels-photo-31985625.jpeg?auto=compress&amp;cs=tinysrgb&amp;dpr=2&amp;h=650&amp;w=940" alt="Freshly picked aroma hop cones (illustrative image)" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>Freshly picked aroma hop cones (illustrative image) — Photo: Ben Young (Pexels)</figcaption></figure>

The beer is available in limited quantities, and will be sold only at souvenir shops in major JR West stations such as Kyoto, Shin-Osaka, Osaka and Hakata, and through the JR West official online shop. No overseas sales have been announced at present. Even so, there are two points about this product that European readers may find interesting. The first is that Echigo Beer, a pioneer of Japanese craft beer, is tackling a traditional Czech style using European hops. You might enjoy sipping a Bohemian pilsner you have at hand while imagining the "light, aromatic and crisp" taste that a Japanese brewery is aiming for with the same ingredients. The second is the collector culture, unique to Japan, of combining railways with drinks. The latest information is due to be posted on the official Instagram account run by JR West Japan Business (@trainbox_jrwt).
