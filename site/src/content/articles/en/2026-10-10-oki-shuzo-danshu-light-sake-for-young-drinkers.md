Oki Shuzo, a brewery on the Oki Islands (Oki-shoto) floating in the Sea of Japan off the coast of Shimane Prefecture, released a new sake called "Danshu?" on 7 July 2026. It is aimed at young people who have just reached 20, the legal drinking age in Japan. The alcohol content is a light 12%, and it comes in a compact 300 ml bottle. It is sold only through the brewery's official online shop. It was designed for occasions where sake has rarely appeared until now, such as toasts at clubs and parties.

## The idea of "brewing the atmosphere"

The product starts from a concept the brewery calls "Vibe-Brew". The word combines the English "vibe" (the mood of a moment) with "brew", and it expresses the idea of brewing the very atmosphere of a pleasant time spent with other people. The concept is a "resonance sake" in which people and the mood of the moment echo one another. The brewery wants it to suit places where music is playing, casual home parties and a first drink with friends, and to serve as a doorway to enjoying sake more freely.

## Well chilled, like a shot

The brewery describes the taste as light, and says that 12% is an alcohol level that is easy to enjoy casually. The recommendation is to keep it well chilled in the fridge until just before drinking and to enjoy it ice-cold. The brewery also suggests using the first glass for a toast, in the manner of a shot.

At the same time, it carries on the brewing philosophy that Oki Shuzo has long cherished: "what goes with plain white rice goes with this sake too". Its soft, unassertive mouthfeel is designed to harmonise with the umami of food, so that it fits not only parties but also the dining table.

## A "first drink" for the younger generation

The watchword is "Shall We Danshu?", a light invitation that echoes the English "Shall we dance?". The brewery positions this bottle as a "first drink" through which young people who know little about sake can encounter sake culture and gradually widen their interest to the history of the Oki Islands and the brewery's more serious sake. It is an example of a traditional brewery rethinking alcohol content, volume and even the occasion for drinking in order to reach new drinkers.

For European readers, "Danshu?" itself is currently sold only through the official online shop in Japan, and no overseas expansion has been announced. Even so, the move offers some ideas on how to enjoy sake. Sake is often thought of as something to drink with food, like wine, but serving a light, lower-strength sake well chilled in a small glass for a toast is another way to enjoy it. When choosing at a specialist shop, look at the alcohol content on the label and try a lighter one served cold. Pairing it with dishes that go well with rice, such as grilled fish or dishes made with dashi, should also make it easier to appreciate the character of such a gentle sake.
