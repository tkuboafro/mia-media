On 23 October 2026, alcohol buyers from Ukraine and Poland held business talks with 10 sake breweries in Nara Prefecture, in the cities of Nara and Kashihara. Nara Prefecture is an inland prefecture in the Kansai region, to the east of Osaka. The meetings were organised by the Japan External Trade Organization (JETRO) to widen overseas sales channels for distinctive, high-quality sake, and the buyers toured three prefectures in Kansai.

## Different rice is like different grape varieties

Stanislav Lapin (37), from Kyiv, works mainly in the spirits division of Wine Bureau, a Ukrainian wine importer and wholesaler. He holds an advanced qualification from the wine education body WSET and usually travels around Europe, but this was his first visit to Japan. At a hotel in Nara, he checked the colour of the sake and described its aroma as "subtly fruity and clear". What surprised him most was a comparison of sakes made in exactly the same way, with only the rice changed. He learned that the rice makes a big difference to the finished sake, and said it was just like wine, where the grape variety, such as Merlot or Cabernet Sauvignon, changes the taste.

## What buyers want: lightness and bubbles

According to Lapin, Japanese restaurants serving sushi, wagyu and other dishes are growing in popularity in Ukraine, and sake consumption is rising with them. Younger people and wealthy consumers are especially keen, and high-priced sake also sells well, he said. At the same time, he pointed out that alcohol consumption is falling worldwide and drinkers are leaning towards lower-strength drinks, and said that in the Ukrainian market he sees the greatest potential in low-alcohol sparkling sake.

## Connecting supply routes through Belgium

<figure class="embed embed--image"><img src="https://images.pexels.com/photos/20581299/pexels-photo-20581299.jpeg?auto=compress&amp;cs=tinysrgb&amp;dpr=2&amp;h=650&amp;w=940" alt="A long transport route combining sea freight and road haulage" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>A long transport route combining sea freight and road haulage — Photo: Wolfgang Weiser (Pexels)</figcaption></figure>

With Russia's invasion continuing, attacks on logistics hubs and infrastructure have carried on this year too. Since 2022 the company has avoided Ukrainian ports, shipping goods by cargo vessel to Belgium and delivering them from there by lorry. When air-raid warnings reach the highest level, supermarkets stop trading and staff have to take shelter. Even so, Lapin said he will keep searching for products with potential in order to support the company's roughly 1,000 employees and their families.

If you would like to enjoy this story in Europe, start by looking for a lower-strength sparkling sake at an import shop or sake bar. It is a style that works well as an aperitif or with lighter dishes such as sushi. Another idea is to compare sakes made by the same brewery with only the rice variety changed. The rice name on the label can help you read the flavour, much like the grape variety on a bottle of wine.
