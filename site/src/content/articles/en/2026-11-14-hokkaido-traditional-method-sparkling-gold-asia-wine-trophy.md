Hokkaido Wine Co., Ltd., based in Otaru, Hokkaido, has won gold medals for two bottle-fermented sparkling wines at the Asia Wine Trophy 2026, an international wine competition held in Daejeon, South Korea. The wines are made using the same method as Champagne, in Japan's northernmost wine-producing region. According to the company, this is the first time a Hokkaido wine has won an award at this competition. The company also received a total of eight awards at the Global Wine & Spirits Awards Asia 2026 in Hong Kong.

## Two gold medallists from a blind-tasting competition

<figure class="embed embed--image"><img src="https://prcdn.freetls.fastly.net/release_image/140719/41/140719-41-e96e16ab1fe348b2eefef0449062f5d4-2274x561.jpg?width=1200&amp;height=900" alt="The &quot;Traditional Method Hokkaido&quot; series, which won gold at the Asia Wine Trophy 2026" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>The &quot;Traditional Method Hokkaido&quot; series, which won gold at the Asia Wine Trophy 2026 — Image: PR TIMES（北海道ワイン） (press release)</figcaption></figure>

The Asia Wine Trophy is organised by DWM of Germany and is officially recognised by the International Organisation of Vine and Wine (OIV). This year, more than 3,500 wines from 35 countries were entered, and over 130 judges assessed them in fully blind tastings. Under OIV rules, medals may be awarded to no more than the top 30% of entries. The gold medals went to two wines from the "Traditional Method Hokkaido" series. Hokkaido Wine has been working seriously with this method since 2016, and the awards reflect recognition of that effort.

## Blanc de Blancs and Rosé: how each is made

<figure class="embed embed--image"><img src="https://prcdn.freetls.fastly.net/release_image/140719/41/140719-41-b38dc894a852d654201ebc27a56b6853-1400x2480.jpg?width=1200&amp;height=900" alt="&quot;Type C Blanc de Blancs&quot;, made from Pinot Blanc and Chardonnay" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>&quot;Type C Blanc de Blancs&quot;, made from Pinot Blanc and Chardonnay — Image: PR TIMES（北海道ワイン） (press release)</figcaption></figure>

"Type C Blanc de Blancs" is a dry (Brut) wine made solely from Pinot Blanc (53%) and Chardonnay (47%) grown at the company's own Tsurunuma Winery vineyard in Urausu, in the Sorachi region. After bottling, it is aged for 40 months until disgorgement (dégorgement). Aromas of green apple, white flowers and citrus are layered with the toasty notes that come from the yeast, and the taut acidity typical of a cool climate supports the palate.

<figure class="embed embed--image"><img src="https://prcdn.freetls.fastly.net/release_image/140719/41/140719-41-cab5698dd2379905b6eb0812a5b9c2bb-1400x2480.jpg?width=1200&amp;height=900" alt="&quot;Type M Rosé&quot;, a dry rosé blended from black grapes" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>&quot;Type M Rosé&quot;, a dry rosé blended from black grapes — Image: PR TIMES（北海道ワイン） (press release)</figcaption></figure>

"Type M Rosé" is a rosé blended from several black grape varieties grown at the company's own vineyard and by contract growers. Aromas of wild strawberry and cherry are joined by a richness that comes from malolactic fermentation (a fermentation that softens the acidity) and ageing in the bottle. It also won gold at the Berliner Wine Trophy, held in Germany in August 2026, making this its second gold medal. Both wines are certified under the "GI Hokkaido" geographical indication, which is given to wines made exclusively from grapes grown in Hokkaido.

The Hong Kong competition is known for judging with an emphasis on Asian food culture and consumer tastes. There, awards went to wines including a 2022 Pinot Noir made from grapes grown at Tazaki Farm in Yoichi, and a 2025 Gewurztraminer from Tsurunuma.

## Enjoying them in Europe

<figure class="embed embed--image"><img src="https://images.pexels.com/photos/18136135/pexels-photo-18136135.jpeg?auto=compress&amp;cs=tinysrgb&amp;dpr=2&amp;h=650&amp;w=940" alt="Sparkling wine paired with oysters" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>Sparkling wine paired with oysters — Photo: Valeria Boltneva (Pexels)</figcaption></figure>

Although the volume of Japanese wine sold in Europe is still small, the "GI Hokkaido" mark on the label is proof that it was made from Hokkaido grapes. Pinot Blanc and Zweigelt, familiar in Germany and Austria, are also grown at around 43 degrees north in Hokkaido. Tasting these wines alongside bottles from your own familiar regions may reveal how the grapes differ. The Blanc de Blancs goes well with oysters and white fish, and the rosé with salmon and light meat dishes, letting you enjoy their character as sparkling wines to drink with food.
