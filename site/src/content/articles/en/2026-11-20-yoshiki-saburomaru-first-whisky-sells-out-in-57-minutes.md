Japanese musician YOSHIKI spent about three years co-developing his first whisky, "Y by YOSHIKI × SABUROMARU", with Saburomaru Distillery in Tonami, Toyama Prefecture. Early sales opened at noon on 20 November 2026 on the online shop of Wakatsuru Shuzo, the company that runs the distillery, and all three early-release editions sold out in just 57 minutes. The release date is YOSHIKI's own birthday. With this, the brand moves into whisky after wine and champagne, and overseas sales are planned for the future.

## Two editions gone in 7 minutes, all three in 57

The first to sell out were the blended whisky and the single cask matured in bourbon barrels, seven minutes after sales began. The remaining edition, a single cask matured in Spanish oak barrels previously used for oloroso sherry, also sold out at 12:57. YOSHIKI commented, "I was surprised on my birthday."

There are three products in the line-up. "Blended Whisky 2026 Edition", blended by YOSHIKI himself, is a 46% ABV whisky made from malt and grain, priced at 13,200 yen including tax, with 6,000 bottles produced. There are two five-year-old single casks: one matured in a bourbon barrel (59%, 235 bottles) and one in an oloroso barrel (58%, 626 bottles), both priced at 77,000 yen including tax. A single cask is a whisky bottled solely from the spirit of one barrel, and its flavour is said to be difficult to reproduce.

## Working with the oldest distillery in Hokuriku

Saburomaru Distillery is run by Wakatsuru Shuzo, founded in 1862. It has been making whisky since 1952 and is the oldest distillery in the Hokuriku region (the Sea of Japan side of central Honshu). It has won numerous awards at the World Whisky Awards in the UK. At the US competition TOP SHELF, a whisky made from a "sister cask" filled at the same time as the oloroso cask used for this release was also named Best of Class, the top prize in the Japanese whisky category. YOSHIKI visited the distillery in Toyama and, together with Takahiko Inagaki, president and master blender of Wakatsuru Shuzo, selected the spirits and casks and decided the blend.

"Y by YOSHIKI" is a wine brand that YOSHIKI launched in 2009 with Rob Mondavi Jr., a winemaker in California's Napa Valley. In 2019, 25,000 bottles of a new wine shipped within two weeks of release. In 2022, he released his first champagne under a joint brand with the French champagne house POMMERY, and the first 10,000 bottles sold out on the same day. This whisky is the first drink he has made with a Japanese producer.

The release date and retailers for Europe have not yet been announced, so the safest course is to keep an eye on the brand's official website and on specialist shops that stock Japanese whisky. It is also worth remembering the name Saburomaru Distillery, which should help you when looking for Japanese craft whisky.
